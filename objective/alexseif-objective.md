# Professional Objective & Profile Strategy

## 1. Targeted Title
Software Architect & NGO Technical Partner

## 2. Objectives — Tiered

**Primary:** Represent 20+ years of software architecture experience honestly and completely, including projects previously undocumented.

**Secondary:** Secure a permanent Software Architect or Principal Engineer role in the Netherlands or EU, with relocation.

**Third:** Attract NGOs seeking an embedded Technical Partner for secure web portals, data sovereignty compliance, legacy system rescue, and staff technical capability building.

## 3. Core Narrative

**Tagline:** "I build the systems behind the systems — and I've been doing it for 20 years."

I am a Software Architect & NGO Technical Partner operating at the intersection of architecture ownership and technical problem solving. I solve the hard problems that come before the code — understanding what's actually needed, designing how it fits together, and building systems that survive contact with reality. 

I've spent 20 years doing this under real constraints — air-gapped government ministries, fragile bank networks, national payment gateways under distributed load, and regulatory frameworks that couldn't be argued with. 

Most people come to me when they need to build something that doesn't exist yet, when they have something that works but can't scale or change, or when they need someone to own the technical function instead of just advising on it. My approach is approachable, human, and honest ("Whatever the problem is, I've probably solved a harder one.").

What I don't do: manual testing. I design the quality gates, define the validation requirements, and establish the QA process. I don't man the gates myself.

## 5. What Energizes Me

Walking into a room — technical or non-technical — where a real problem exists, understanding how things currently work, and designing the system that solves it. The requirement-to-schema moment. The API contract before a line is written. The infrastructure decision that determines whether the system survives its first year of production.

## 6. What I Won't Do

Own QA execution. I'll design the test strategy, define validators, establish the process, and specify what coverage looks like. I will not be the person running the tests.

## 7. Success Metric

One offer. The right offer. Senior enough to use the architecture capability, honest enough to match the documented history, and meaningful enough to be worth the next 20 years.
