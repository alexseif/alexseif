# Full-Stack Web Developer | EMICS | 2017

## Context & Scale
Founded in Alexandria in 2009, EMICS (Egyptian Marine Insurance Consultations & Services) provides specialized marine surveying, risk assessment, and claims consultancy across Egyptian ports, including Alexandria, Port Said, Damietta, and the Suez Canal. The company had an outdated digital presence that failed to communicate its specialized maritime services clearly. Alex Seif was independently contracted to redesign and restructure the company website, improving information architecture, readability, and administrative maintainability.

## Architectural Decisions
* **Content Restructuring:** Reorganized complex marine insurance and technical surveying offerings into clear, navigable service categories, eliminating cluttered legacy page hierarchies.
* **Responsive Layout Migration:** Replaced the non-responsive legacy layout with a fluid mobile-first design, ensuring clear legibility for maritime adjusters and shipowners accessing the site on mobile devices and shipboard connections.
* **Administrative Simplification:** Standardized the WordPress administrative backend so internal non-technical staff could update company news, branch details, and contact points without developer assistance.

## Engineering Execution
* **Frontend:** Built responsive CSS3 and semantic HTML5 page templates ensuring legibility across standard resolutions and cellular connections.
* **CMS & Backend:** Custom WordPress theme implementation in PHP, streamlining navigation menus and contact inquiry forms.
* **Data Layer & Hosting:** MySQL database on a Linux/Apache host, configuring basic caching headers and clean rewrite rules.

## Measurable Impact
* Clarified service presentation across Alexandria and regional canal port operations for international and domestic maritime partners.
* Improved mobile accessibility and page rendering speed over variable port cellular networks.
* Provided complete administrative independence to internal staff for ongoing content maintenance.
