# Lead Full-Stack Engineer & Web Architect | NileTech (Turathuna Abu Dhabi) | 2017

## Context & Scale
The Cultural Programs and Heritage Festivals Committee in Abu Dhabi is the government entity responsible for preserving, documenting, and promoting Emirati national heritage through major cultural festivals, initiatives, and broadcast media productions. Contracted through digital agency NileTech, Alex Seif served as Lead Full-Stack Engineer and Architect to build the committee's official digital platform, Turathuna (turathuna.ae). The platform required government-grade stability, rigorous Arabic and English multilingual workflows, and dedicated modules to showcase seasonal festival schedules and broadcast television programming.

## Architectural Decisions
* **Enterprise Multilingual Drupal Architecture:** Implemented Drupal multilingual entity translation, ensuring synchronous editorial parity, separate SEO-optimized URL paths, and bidirectional layout handling (Arabic right-to-left RTL and English left-to-right LTR).
* **Broadcast TV Program & Episode Taxonomy:** Designed custom content entities and taxonomy schemas to catalog broadcast television programs produced by the committee, organizing episodes, video assets, and promotional air dates within an intuitive media archive.
* **Chronological Event Lifecycle Engine:** Structured an automated event management system segregating upcoming festival calendars from historical event archives, automatically shifting active programs to archive status based on timestamp triggers.

## Engineering Execution
* **Bidirectional Frontend Templates:** Developed modular HTML5 and CSS3 templates with native RTL stylesheets, ensuring crisp typographic hierarchy in Arabic calligraphy fonts and fluid mobile responsiveness.
* **Custom Drupal Entity Views:** Built optimized database queries and cached Drupal Views for high-traffic listing pages (festivals, broadcast schedules, media press releases).
* **Security & Infrastructure Hardening:** Configured server-level security headers, database sanitization, and reverse-proxy caching on a Linux production environment meeting UAE government hosting specifications.

## Measurable Impact
* Successfully deployed the bilingual portal in production ahead of major UAE cultural festival cycles with zero critical launch issues.
* Streamlined editorial workflows for government communications staff, enabling simultaneous publishing of news, television media, and event programs in both Arabic and English.
* Established an authoritative, enduring digital archive for Emirati cultural heritage initiatives that remains in active production.
