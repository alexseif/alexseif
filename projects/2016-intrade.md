# Solo Full-Stack Developer | Intrade (2016)

## Overview
Intrade is an international import and export trading enterprise managing global commodity distribution and cross-border trade operations. The business required a structured digital platform to showcase its multi-category product lines, technical specifications, and downloadable commercial brochures to B2B partners worldwide.

Contracted independently as Solo Full-Stack Developer to design, build, and deploy the entire solution. Built a categorized B2B catalog on WordPress, optimized for international browsing speeds, low-bandwidth trade terminals, and straightforward inquiry routing for wholesale buyers.

## Key Technologies & Tools
* **B2B Product Catalog Architecture:** Structured hierarchical taxonomies and specification tables for commercial trade goods and commodity categories.
* **Brochure & Specification Downloads:** Implemented optimized asset delivery for downloadable PDF brochures, product data sheets, and enterprise documentation.
* **Lead Generation & Inquiries:** Configured custom RFQ and commercial inquiry workflows routed directly to trade desk officers.
* **Performance & Lightweight Delivery:** Optimized asset payloads and server caching to ensure responsive access over variable international network connections.
