# Technical Advisor & Software Architect | Information Technology Institute (ITI) | 2017

## Context & Scale
The Information Technology Institute (ITI), affiliated with Egypt's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT), provides advanced technical training and incubation programs for the nation's software engineering talent. ITI invited Alex Seif as an industry expert to deliver targeted technical workshops and hands-on exercises for aspiring founders and engineering graduates from diverse backgrounds preparing to launch tech ventures.

## Workshop Curriculum & Architecture Mentorship
* **Pragmatic MVP Scoping:** Guided participants through separating core functional requirements from speculative feature wishlists, preventing premature architectural overengineering before market validation.
* **Uncovering Hidden Technical Debt:** Taught practical techniques for identifying overlooked operational complexities, including data validation, background processing queues, transactional email delivery, security safeguards, and continuous deployment overhead.
* **Feasibility & Financial Modeling:** Trained founders to evaluate technical architecture choices against financial realities, balancing infrastructure hosting tiers, third-party API dependencies, and maintenance costs against projected early revenue.

## Hands-On Exercises & Delivery
* **Architecture Roadmap Exercises:** Led collaborative system blueprinting sessions where participants mapped data flows, identified database bottlenecks, and selected suitable technology stacks for their specific domain constraints.
* **Budget & Runway Stress-Testing:** Conducted risk-modeling drills requiring founders to defend their technical architecture and cost projections against hypothetical user spikes and funding constraints.
* **Cross-Discipline Alignment:** Facilitated exercises designed to align non-technical founders with technical leads, establishing common vocabulary around sprint planning, acceptance criteria, and technical debt.

## Measurable Impact
* Mentored dozens of prospective founders through structured feasibility reviews, helping them establish lean, deployable technical architectures.
* Prevented critical early-stage misallocations of capital by teaching engineering students how to build within tight infrastructure budgets.
* Strengthened ITI startup incubation outcomes by instilling practical architectural discipline and realistic scoping practices.
