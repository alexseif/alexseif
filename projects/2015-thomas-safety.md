# Full-Stack DeveloperFull-Stack Developer | Thomas Safety (2015)

## Overview
Thomas Safety is an industrial equipment provider specializing in occupational health, personal protective equipment (PPE), and workplace safety supplies. The company contracted a modern web presence to organize its extensive product catalog and streamline incoming quotation inquiries from industrial procurement managers.

Served as Full-Stack DeveloperFull-Stack Developer across the complete engagement. Gathered business requirements directly from company leadership, engineered a clean, categorized product showcase on WordPress, and provisioned a secured cloud hosting environment on DigitalOcean.

## Key Technologies & Tools
* **B2B Product Catalog Architecture:** Structured hierarchical product taxonomies for safety footwear, protective apparel, fire safety gear, and industrial hardware.
* **Request for Quote (RFQ) Workflows:** Built verified inquiry forms allowing procurement clients to request volume pricing on specific SKU categories.
* **Cloud Hosting & Infrastructure:** Deployed and hardened an isolated Linux server on DigitalOcean with automated backup schedules and fast Nginx caching.
